What matters before you choose

Match battery voltage, chemistry, capacity, condition, charging mode, temperature limits, and vehicle manufacturer guidance.

A maintainer is not a universal repair for a damaged, frozen, leaking, swollen, internally shorted, or otherwise unsafe battery.

Connect and disconnect in the manufacturer sequence, provide ventilation, avoid ignition sources, and use the correct mode before leaving a charger unattended.
