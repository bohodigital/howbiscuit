How to Clean a Window Air Conditioner Filter

Turn the air conditioner off and unplug it, remove the filter according to the model’s manual, vacuum loose dust or wash the filter if it is reusable, let it dry completely, and reinstall it in the correct orientation.

Updated Aug 2, 2026 4 min read Researched Not hands-on tested

What to know

  • The owner’s manual controls because filter location, material, and cleaning method vary by model.
  • Wash only filters identified as reusable or washable by the manufacturer.
  • Replace torn, warped, crumbling, moldy, or disposable filters rather than trying to clean them.

Turn off the air conditioner and unplug it before removing the filter. Follow the model’s manual to release the front grille or filter panel. Vacuum loose dust or wash the filter with mild soap and lukewarm water if the manufacturer identifies it as washable. Let it dry completely before reinstalling it.

Filter location, material, and cleaning interval vary by model, so the owner’s manual outranks generic advice. LG, Frigidaire, and GE all direct users to turn off or unplug the unit and follow model-specific removal instructions. [1] [2] [3]

What you need

  • A vacuum with a soft brush attachment
  • A sink or basin
  • Mild dish soap
  • Cool or lukewarm water
  • A soft cloth
  • The air conditioner’s manual

Avoid bleach, solvents, abrasive scrubbers, boiling water, pressure washers, and dishwashers unless the manufacturer explicitly permits them.

Turn the unit off and unplug it

Use the power button, then disconnect the plug from the wall outlet. Do not remove the filter while the fan is operating.

If the plug, cord, outlet, or power-control device is hot, discolored, cracked, wet, or damaged, stop and address the electrical problem rather than proceeding with routine cleaning.

Find and remove the filter

Most window units place the filter behind the front grille, in a pull-out slot near the top, behind a hinged intake panel, or behind a removable front cover.

Do not force the panel. Plastic tabs become brittle with age and react poorly to the traditional human repair technique of pulling harder. Photograph the filter’s orientation before removal if the airflow direction is not obvious.

Remove loose dust

For a lightly dirty reusable filter, use a vacuum’s soft brush attachment. Support the filter so the mesh does not bend or tear. Vacuuming may be sufficient between deeper washings when the filter carries only a thin layer of lint.

Clean the removable grille according to the manual, but do not push tools or moisture into electrical controls or the fan compartment.

Wash it only if it is reusable

If the manual permits washing:

  1. Rinse with cool or lukewarm water.
  2. Add a small amount of mild soap when needed.
  3. Gently loosen dirt without stretching the mesh.
  4. Rinse until the water runs clear.
  5. Shake off excess water without twisting the frame.

LG instructs users of supported models to use lukewarm water below 104°F, while Frigidaire recommends warm, soapy water for its washable room-air-conditioner filters. [1] [2]

Do not wash disposable fiber filters, activated-carbon inserts unless permitted, electronic or specialty filters without model instructions, or torn and deteriorating material.

Let the filter dry completely

Place the filter in a ventilated shaded area. Do not reinstall it dripping wet, and do not speed drying with an oven, hair dryer, direct flame, or high heat. Frigidaire specifically directs owners to let the filter dry completely before reinstallation. [2]

A wet filter can shed water into the unit, trap more dust, or distort if exposed to heat.

Reinstall it correctly

Return the filter in its original orientation and close the panel securely. Plug the unit back in only after the filter and surrounding surfaces are dry.

Some air conditioners display a filter reminder based on operating hours. Reset the reminder only after the filter has actually been inspected and cleaned. The exact interval and reset procedure are model-specific.

How often should you clean it?

Inspect the filter regularly during heavy cooling season. GE recommends every 30 days for many room-air-conditioner models, while other products use different schedules. Pets, smoke, construction dust, open windows, and continuous operation can make cleaning necessary sooner. [3]

Use the manual’s interval as the baseline and the filter’s visible condition as the practical trigger.

When the filter should be replaced

Replace the filter when it is torn, punctured, warped, crumbling, shedding, permanently clogged, moldy within the material, the wrong size, or identified by the manufacturer as disposable.

A dirty filter can restrict airflow and contribute to poor cooling or icing. Cleaning it does not repair a refrigerant, compressor, sensor, drainage, or installation problem.

Bottom line

Unplug the air conditioner, remove the filter according to its manual, vacuum or gently wash it if reusable, dry it fully, and reinstall it correctly. Replace damaged or disposable filters instead of attempting to resurrect them with increasingly inventive chemicals.

Limits and cautions

  • The article does not authorize opening the sealed cabinet or handling refrigerant components.
  • Cleaning intervals vary by model, use, pets, smoke, and dust conditions.
  • A clean filter cannot repair a compressor, refrigerant, sensor, drainage, or installation problem.
