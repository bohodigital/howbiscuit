How to Clean and Drain a Portable Air Conditioner Safely

Portable air conditioners remove moisture while cooling. Some evaporate most condensate through the exhaust, while others require occasional or continuous draining depending on mode and humidity.

Updated Aug 5, 2026 4 min read Researched Not hands-on tested
Disclosure

This article contains no paid or affiliate links.

Read the disclosure

What to know

  • Portable air conditioners remove moisture while cooling. Some evaporate most condensate through the exhaust, while others require occasional or continuous draining depending on mode and humidity.
  • Unplug the unit, clean washable filters, drain the correct port, reinstall every cap, and keep the exhaust route open.

Turn the unit off and unplug it.

Remove the washable filter, vacuum or wash it according to the manual, and dry it completely.

Drain retained water through the exact port specified for the operating mode.

GE, LG, Frigidaire, and Whynter all use model-specific filter and drainage procedures, so the owner’s manual controls.

Find the model instructions

Record:

  • Manufacturer
  • Model
  • Filter location
  • Filter type
  • Upper drain purpose
  • Lower drain purpose
  • Continuous-drain procedure
  • Pump feature
  • Storage procedure
  • Error-code meanings

A portable unit may include:

  • One or several washable mesh filters
  • Replaceable carbon filter
  • Upper drain for dehumidification
  • Lower drain for complete emptying
  • Condensate pump
  • Self-evaporation system

Do not connect a hose to a convenient-looking opening and hope drainage will respect the enthusiasm.

Disconnect power

Use the control panel to turn the appliance off.

Unplug it by gripping the plug.

Do not clean or drain the machine when:

  • Plug is hot
  • Cord is damaged
  • Receptacle is wet
  • Water reached controls
  • Cabinet is cracked
  • Burning odor is present

Keep the unit upright.

Some manufacturers require several hours upright after substantial tilting or transport before restarting so refrigerant and compressor oil can settle. Whynter warns users to transport supported units upright or leave them upright before operation.

Clean the washable filter

A typical supported process is:

  1. Remove the rear filter cover.
  2. Slide out the mesh filter.
  3. Vacuum it with a soft attachment.
  4. Wash severe dirt with lukewarm water and neutral detergent where permitted.
  5. Rinse.
  6. Dry completely in shade.
  7. Reinstall.

LG instructs users to unplug portable units, clean severe contamination with neutral detergent, avoid water above 104°F, and dry the filter in shade.

Do not use:

  • Dishwasher
  • Clothes dryer
  • Hair dryer
  • Bleach
  • Solvent
  • Abrasive brush
  • Direct flame

Replace specialty filters as instructed

Carbon or odor filters may be replaceable rather than washable.

Whynter recommends model-specific replacement intervals and distinguishes washable air filters from carbon filters.

Do not wash a carbon insert simply because it sits beside a washable screen.

Drain the lower tank

When the display indicates a full tray or the unit is being stored:

  1. Unplug the machine.
  2. Roll it to a level water-resistant area.
  3. Place a shallow pan beneath the lower rear drain.
  4. Remove the cap.
  5. Remove the rubber plug.
  6. Let the water drain.
  7. Reinstall the plug.
  8. Tighten the cap.
  9. Dry the floor.

GE recommends a shallow pan of at least approximately 1.5 quarts for supported models. Frigidaire identifies a P1 code as a full bottom tray requiring drainage.

Do not remove the plug while the machine sits on carpet.

Continuous drainage

Some models allow a hose on an upper or dedicated port during:

  • Dehumidifier mode
  • Heat mode
  • High-humidity operation

The hose normally needs a continuous downward slope unless the unit contains a pump.

Check:

  • Hose diameter
  • Kinks
  • Drain height
  • Secure connection
  • Floor-drain capacity
  • Leak risk

Gravity water will not climb upward merely because the hose eventually reaches a sink.

Clean the exterior

Use a soft damp cloth.

Keep water away from:

  • Controls
  • Plug
  • Cord
  • Air outlet
  • Internal coil
  • Electrical openings

Do not spray household cleaner directly onto the cabinet.

Inspect the exhaust system

Check:

  • Hose not crushed
  • Hose not torn
  • Window adapter sealed
  • Intake unobstructed
  • Exhaust outlet clear
  • Curtains away from grilles

Whynter warns that blocked filters and bent or crushed exhaust hoses interfere with normal operation.

Do not extend the exhaust hose beyond the manufacturer’s permitted length.

Seasonal storage

Before storage:

  1. Drain all water.
  2. Clean and dry filters.
  3. Run an approved fan-only drying period if the manual instructs it.
  4. Remove remote batteries.
  5. Coil the cord loosely.
  6. Store upright.
  7. Protect from freezing and pests.

A portable air conditioner stored with water inside can develop odor, deposits, or biological growth during the off-season.

Bottom line

Unplug the unit, clean washable filters, drain the correct port, reinstall every cap, and keep the exhaust route open.

The filter and condensate system are owner maintenance. The sealed refrigerant circuit, compressor, wiring, and internal fan are not invitations for exploratory disassembly.
