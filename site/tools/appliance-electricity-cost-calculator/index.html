Appliance Electricity Cost Calculator

Calculate appliance cost per use, month, and year from watts and time, measured kWh, or annual EnergyGuide kWh. Your entries stay in your browser.

Private by design: appliance names, rates, state choices, inputs, and results are not sent or stored.

Check these fields:

    Electricity price

    May 2026 U.S. residential average. Your utility rate may be very different.

    State averages are EIA statewide monthly averages, not your utility tariff. Choosing a state does not use location services.

    Calculation method

    Use only a compatible meter within its voltage, current, and wattage ratings. Ordinary plug-in meters are not for standard 240-volt or hardwired appliances.

    Estimated electricity cost

    Enter the active method’s values, then calculate. Nothing is transmitted.

    May 2026 U.S. average

    Watts-and-time assumes the entered power for the entered time. Thermostats, compressors, heaters, and sensors may cycle.

    This estimate includes energy priced per kWh. It does not remove fixed customer charges and may not capture taxes, demand charges, tiered rates, time-of-use periods, water, gas, or fuel.

    Formulas and worked examples

    Watts and time: kWh per use = (watts ÷ 1,000) × (minutes ÷ 60). At 1,500 W for 10 minutes, energy is 0.25 kWh.

    Measured kWh: cost per use = measured kWh × price per kWh. At 0.5 kWh and 18.44¢/kWh, cost is $0.0922.

    Annual kWh: annual cost = annual kWh × price per kWh. At 400 kWh/year and 18.44¢/kWh, cost is $73.76/year.
